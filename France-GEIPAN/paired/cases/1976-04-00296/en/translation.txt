Description: On April 1, 1976, around 17:20, several people observed in the sky a white streak that seemed to plunge into the sea. They then see a gray smoke mass that persists over the water for a quarter of an hour before completely disappearing. According to the gendarmerie investigation, no aircraft from the airport was in flight after 5 PM. She will not collect any objects or debris of any kind on the coastline. It is likely that the witnesses observed the fall into the sea of an object, but we lack information to identify it.
