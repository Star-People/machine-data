Summary: Observation of a flying object following a road.
--- seg-9b09062eeffc97b4316a5740 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 10, 1976, around 4:30 a.m., a driver noticed the presence at low altitude above the road of a craft he did not recognize, which was preceding him. The witness described an object 4 to 5 meters long and approximately 1 meter in diameter. It was a bright red color and had a black band running the length of it. The upper part seemed to be topped with a cabin and a blinking light. The driver stopped to observe the craft, which appeared to be moving backward as if approaching the witness. Frightened, the witness immediately resumed his journey. The gendarmerie collected no further information.
