Description: On October 19, 1976, at approximately 23:40, a family in a car observed in the sky a peculiar object with light beams at each end. This object emitted lights towards the ground, then rose after turning off its beams, and disappeared towards the East. A person whose residence is located in the valley where the observation took place noticed disturbances on his television set around 23:30 but made no direct observation. No information was gathered from the various services contacted (Weather, radar service, EDF, water treatment plants, and nearby balancing stations operating day and night) to validate any hypothesis.
