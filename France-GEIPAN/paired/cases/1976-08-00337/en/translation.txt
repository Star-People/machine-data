Description: On August 28, 1976, around 4:30 a.m., a witness observed in the overcast sky the passage of a luminous phenomenon. A bright red ball passed quickly and silently overhead and disappeared, hidden by the landscape. The witness's dog was frightened. Later, this person saw a white glow in the shape of a half-circle, motionless behind a curtain of trees in the direction of the phenomenon's disappearance. No trace was found at the scene, and no other testimony about this phenomenon was reported, for which we lack information.
