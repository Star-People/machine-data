--- seg-d31031607d78406af52dfde4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Following an article in the local press (not present in the archives GEIPAN), several people testified at the gendarmerie about their observations made in early January 1976 (the date of January 5 is indicated for the first observation). Each witness observed a particular luminous phenomenon or object at different days, hours, and locations. One of the witnesses said he saw a man emerge from a craft sitting on the ground and walk towards him. Scared, the witness ran away. He said he had the same encounter the next day. The gendarmerie will find no trace or evidence at the scene that confirms or refutes the brief statements of the witnesses.
