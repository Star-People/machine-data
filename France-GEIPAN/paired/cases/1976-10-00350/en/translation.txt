Description: On October 7, 1976, several teenagers observed the quick passage of an object above the neighborhood's houses around 7 PM. The object is described as having the shape of a cigar that is red in color and moves silently along a trajectory from southeast to northwest. No adults will be witnesses to this phenomenon, which will be reported in the local press on the insistence of one of the parents. These children likely made an observation of the passage of an aircraft, as air traffic is heavy in this area near the Metz airport.
