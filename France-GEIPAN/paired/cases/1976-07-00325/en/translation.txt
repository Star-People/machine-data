Description: Between July 26 and July 29, 1976, at different times, several witnesses observed luminous and electrical phenomena that intrigued them. One of the witnesses observed the rapid passage of a flashing green ball in the sky, leaving a red trail. TV reception disruptions and an electric meter fire were noted during those days. The gendarmerie investigation will report several witness accounts of different observations. This case is classified C due to a lack of consistency and information in the testimonies.
