Description: A witness reports, on June 19, 1976, his three sightings of light phenomena between April 1976 and June 1976. On April 7, 1976, around 11:30 PM, he noticed a luminous object in the sky shaped like a cigar: it landed on the path leading to the witness's home and produced a strong light directed in a single direction. During the month of May 1976, around the same time and practically in the same location, he notices two very bright craft, one of which appears to be parked on the nearby path near his home. Waking up his brother and parents immediately, these people observe these luminous phenomena for approximately 1.5 hours without being able to determine a precise shape. No noise is heard. Everything disappears abruptly. One of the witnesses will find traces on the asphalt at the presumed location of the landing. On Friday, June 16, at the same time again, the witness notices a strong brightness on a field near his residence. Following all these observations, he decides to testify. During the gendarmerie investigation, no findings could be made regarding the first two observations due to the witness's late declaration. For the third observation, the landowner will claim that he lit a wood fire in his field that evening to grill.
