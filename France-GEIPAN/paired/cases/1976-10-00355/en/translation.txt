Description: Between October 20 and 26, 1976, in the evening, several people in the same geographic sector observed multicolored lights that seemed to come from an unidentified craft. The witnesses were frightened by these lights and the stationary or moving craft that emitted a deep noise. This phenomenon is classified as C due to a lack of information.
