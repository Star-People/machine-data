Summary: Close observation of the movement of a flying cylindrical object at 10m above ground with a change in direction: unexplained phenomenon.
--- seg-58c4496cb6957b26f4d2f37a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 25, 1976, around 07:10, a farmer hunting observed a red glow in the distance for a few seconds, which he took for a chimney fire. Dominating the landscape, he then sees a sparkling light approaching him that moves according to an E-W trajectory. Upon reaching ten or fifteen meters from the fighter, this phenomenon changes direction, and the witness realizes he is in the presence of an object of elongated shape, approximately two meters long. The cylindrical object is stationary at 10 meters from the ground and an intermittent red glow radiates all around the object. It then moves silently towards the South, stops, resumes its journey, and disappears, hidden by the landscape. Following the gendarmerie investigation, no further testimonies will be collected, and no traces will be found in the area of passage indicated by the witness. It is difficult to conduct a retrospective investigation on a case as old (see investigation notes). The hypothesis of observing an aircraft is not retained. This unexplained case is inconsistent (one witness only). The observation presents a high degree of strangeness (close but short observation 8 to 10 sec). In the current GEIPAN classification, this unexplained, inconsistent case with a strong air of strangeness is classified as PAN D1.
