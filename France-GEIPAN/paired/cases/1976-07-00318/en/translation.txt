Summary: Probable observation of a rising sun reflection phenomenon.
--- seg-17d5429e76c1d6ef6f95fc66 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 13, 1976, at 5:45 a.m., two witnesses, finishing their night shift in a quarry, suddenly observed a stationary white sphere in the sky that progressively enlarged. The observation lasts 4 to 5 seconds then the phenomenon disappears. No noise is heard. According to the gendarmerie investigation, the observers were in the highest point of the region and they had a very wide field of vision. The weather was sunny and there were no clouds. The phenomenon was located vertically above the coast of Vaucouleurs at a distance of approximately 8km. At this date and time, it is likely that these people were witness to a barely risen sun reflection phenomenon.
