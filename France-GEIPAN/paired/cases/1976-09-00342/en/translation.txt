Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon and intense heat sensation during stormy weather. Sudden disappearance. Probable observation of a globular lightning phenomenon.
--- seg-efd3896a24e62d6735f86f71 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 12, 1976, around 10:50 PM, a family observed a luminous phenomenon near their home. At the door step, one of the witnesses felt an intense heat around his home and his attention was drawn to an elliptical shape of incandescing white with a glowing halo. This phenomenon, behind some trees, lasted about two minutes and descended to the ground, disappearing suddenly. A few moments earlier, a disturbance had been noted on the television screen and an animal had been startled abruptly. The next day, the gendarmerie found no traces on the ground in the observation area. A neighbor testified having heard around 11 PM like a thunderclap and noted a power outage for a few minutes but he saw nothing of the phenomenon. This observation case was long classified C due to lack of information. It was recently submitted to an expert on lightning phenomena. The atmospheric conditions at the time of the observation, the description of the phenomenon's behavior, are compatible with the hypothesis of an atmospheric electrical manifestation called ball lightning or globular lightning. This case is classified B: probable observation of a globular lightning phenomenon.
