Description: On January 17, 1976, around 6:00 PM, a farmer observed above the woods the passage of an unrecognized craft. It moved slowly from east to west without any noise being heard. During the observation, which lasted one minute, the witness noticed the activation of a powerful spotlight that scanned the plain. The witness left to get his son, but upon their return, the craft had disappeared. Despite a gendarmerie investigation, no other testimonies or information were gathered.
