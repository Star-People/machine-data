Klemath Falls, Orogon
26 February 1953
Description of Incident
At 2159 PST a round red stationary object was sighted by a CAA operator for
10 minutes time. The object seemed to fade in and fade out intermittently and
eventually faded out completely. The observer estimated the unknown to be at a
70° azimuth from his position and at an altitude of 10,000'.
Weather at the
ime of sighting consisted of scattered clouds at 2,000' with 10 miles visibility.
II. Discussion of Incident
Project Blue Book and itg contract astronomer evaluate this report as defin-
itely caused by the astronomical body Venus. Nany similar reports have been re-
ceived by ATIC during February of an object in the western sky appearing to change
color and fading intermittently, and almost all such reports have been found to
be Venus,
It is interesting to note in this and other similar observations that exper-
lenced CAA and Air Force personnel have been understandably confused by this
bright planet, comparatively isolated, low on the horizon and sometines seen
through a high haze layer causing a rapid change in color. Red is the color
given most often.
III. Conclusion
Was Verus.
