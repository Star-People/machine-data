Declassification Authority NND 923007
30 May 56
CERIIEICATE
At approximately 1930 hours on the night of May 30, 1956, I, A/2C
Walter F. O'Rourke, AF11202909, received a call from Airman Delagardo
at Air Police Hq.
He asked me if I had any planes flying. I told him
that the field had closed at sundown, 1908 hours. He then asked me if
I could tell him what was flying around at the far end of Runway 23. I
told him I would try to find out. When I went out to the ramp, I saw
an object bearing the light markings similar to an H-19, one red light
in the center and one white light on eash side. At times it was going
very slow and then very fast. The altitude would range from about 50
feet up to 3,000 faet.
The object stayed in the area for about two and
one half hours (2½). I went up to the tower to see if I could get a
better look. Even with field glasses, I could not make the object out.
I called Airman Delagardo and told him I had no idea what it was. Then
at about 2130 hours I was again called by the Air Police. They told me
that two state troopers were at Air Police Hqs. They wanted to know
what the object was. I told the APs to send them down to Base Ops so
that I could get a position of the object from them. A short time Later
I was called by the APs and they told me that the state troopers said
forget it. At about 2200 hours, I was called by Miami Coast Guard on
Plan 62.
They wanted to know if I had anything flying. They said that
they had been called by the Homestead Sheriff about a low flying plane.
I then called Miami ATC to see if they had anything in the area.
They
had nothing flying in our area. I then called Flight Service and asked
them what they had coming over our area. They had one plane that had
gone to the Keys. The plane wasn't in our area when the lights were
spotted. Miami ATC' called back and said that they had a plane coming
through our area and that he would have the pilot check to see what he
could find.
The plane checked the area and gave a regater report. ATC
told me to call the dusting companys in Homestead. I called and they
said they had no planes flying. Miami Coast Guard then called back and
said to forget whole thing. Then at 2330 hours when I was coming back
from chow, I saw an object flying between one and three thousand feet.
He flew over the main runway from 5 to 23. He had just one flasing red
light. I went up to the tower and tried to spot him with the aid of
field glasses. The only thing I could see was the red light. The light
was making a rocking movement. He stayed in the area 15 minutes. I
called Flight Service and ATC. They had no planes flying. The night
was very still and no wind. I heard no noise from the object, just
the light.
Walter 70 Rouka
w/20, Homestead ArB, fia
AL 11202909
