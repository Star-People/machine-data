III. SOURCE'S DESCRIPTION OF SIGHTING:
SOURCE'S Statement: "We were at the home of Mr and Mrs G. N. Stanford an
informal outdoor barbecue.
About twelve-twenty-five, Mrs Frehse, who first saw
the object said, Oh! Look!
And at first we all thought it was the landing
lights of a plane and many observations were made. It was an intense brilliant
yellowish light, that to me was like a huge Roman candle that had been shot into
the sky. There was no sound.
The object moved at a moderate rate of speed. The
light turned and moved into the SW direction and we lost the light over the trees.
It was about five minutes later that one of the guests called the GOC to see if
any foreign object had been reported."
Additional Information:
