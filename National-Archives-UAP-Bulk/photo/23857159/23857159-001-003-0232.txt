This investigator has been unable to determine what was responsible for the
sighting by Mrs. Pollard. Experience has shown that the area in which Mrs. Pollard
lives is responsible for the majority of the "Flying Saucer" reports received by
this station. The steel mills in the McKeesport area produce vast quantities of
dust, which, with the proper combination of twilight and artificial illumination

often produce peculiar sights. I consider it very probable that this is what
Mrs. Pollard and Mrs. Dodd saw.
