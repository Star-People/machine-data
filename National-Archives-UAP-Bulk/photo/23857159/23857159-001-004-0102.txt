LUMINOUS WHISTLING OBJECT OBSERVED OVER THE OSLO AREA...Aftenposten II
19 May 1958

A luminous celestial body was observed last night at 2210 hours by
several people in Oslo and surroundings, It was clearly rotund and had a
luminous “tail” trailing behind, It moved at great speed in a direction
from NE to SW, and at “very low altitude”. A married couple in Volden,
Asker, relate that they first heard an even buzz, then a whistle as from
a projeetile, but not in the least resembling the humming of an engine.
It was the queer sound whieh made them look up and they then discovered
a strongly luminant sphere, trailing "sparks" behind. Shortly after-
wards’ this couple had met another married couple who had also heard the
whistling sound without reflecting that it might come from above their
heads. They had not looked up and, therefore, not eaught sight of the
celestial body.

Observer Rolf Brahde of the Solar Observatory at Harestua stated.
to Aftenposten that they, at the time the eelestial body was observed,
