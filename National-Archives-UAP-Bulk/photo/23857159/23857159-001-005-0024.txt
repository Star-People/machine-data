On 21 June 1959, a RB-66 mission FJ-056 at 2100N11312E 2246% sighted
four (4) pairs, total of eight (8), unidentified objects. The EC operator
directed attention to the area by
intercept of a suspected unusual signal. This
signal, upon analysis at the PACAF IPC, has since been determined to be a commer-
cial radar not associated with the objects. Bearing of the signal to abeam the
RB-66 also did not occur until well beyond where the objects were sighted. Ob-
jects were sighted by the pilot and gunner. The objects were described as round
and of a dark color.
The four pairs were spaced over an area of an estimated fifty
(50) miles parallel to the RB-66 course. Separation of the objects in each pair
was estimated at 200 - 300 yards. Upon approach, the second pair moved notice-
ably closer together.
Size was compared to an EW radome, 25-30 feet diameter,
to 100-200 feet diameter.
Shadows were observed approximately three diameters
from the objects. Round shape did not change although RB-66 passed directly over
the objects at 34,000 feet. One ship was sighted approximately 3 - 5 miles off
either end of line of objects.
Wake from the ships was observed although no
wake or other information available on the objects. Ships and objects were mov-
ing in a SW direction from appearance.
On 3 July 1959, another RB-66 crew, mission FJ-061 at 2038N11235E
2343%, a group of sixteen (16) unidentified objects were observed by the pilot.
The gunner observed the group of 16 plus another group of eight (8) objects.
Objects were irregularly grouped in pairs along the flight heading of 033 degrees
covering an area of about five (5) miles.
Seven power binoculars were used dur-
ing observation and objects appeared cream colored, of oval or oblong shape, 40-
60 feet length and 30-40 feet width estimated. Objects were viewed for thirteen
(13) minutes at 36,000 feet.
Objects in each pair were estimated 300 feet apart.
No shadows, wake or other features were observed.
On 4 July 1959, a RB-66, with same crew which made 3 July sighting, mission
FJ-062, at 2038N11235E 2318%, sighted five (5) objects through a 9/10 undercast.
These objects were sighted from 36,000 feet for a three minute period. Objects
appeared to be fishing boats of an estimated sixty (60) feet length, fifteen (15)
feet width.
The pilot and gunner made the sighting at a distance of approximately
Five (5) miles. Objects were described as brown in color although detailed fea-
tures could not be determined.
KD-2 film of mission FJ-056 revealed a radar return on frame 8180 at
2345.02% which shows area and location of theobjects relative to the aircraft.
Film for missions FJ-06l and FJ-062 is forwarded for comparison, although the
latter films do not show the object reflection due to high gain.
Comments of Preparing Officer: Debriefing and further questioning of the
pilots and crews failed to add significant details to information previously re-
ported. Altitude of observation precluded a detailed description even though
the pilots were well experienced in flying in Far East.
