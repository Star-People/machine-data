Copy Nowse. 29,

REPORT NOQ. 2010/61 Date : 14 Jan 60

CHINA (Sinkiang) - A: AIR

Source : A resident of KULDJA, who left that town in Nov 60,

1, Unidentified flying object over Sinkiang Nov 60

At about 8 pm one evening at the beginning of Aug 60, two hunters, on
their journey back to AULDJA from a hunting expedition, had lain on
their backs on the ground resting at a place twenty-two kilometres
north-east of KULDJA. While lying thus, one hunter had noticed a
round light, a little larger than a star and far brighter, rise above
the western horizon and pass directly overhead, finally vanishing
behind the horizon in the east-north-east. The light had taken about
ten minutes to travel from horizon to horizon. It had been reddish
in colour and had had no halo. It had left no trail behind it and the
observer had heard no noise. He had noticed, however, that the stars
surrounding it had been obscured for a moment by its passage, as though
the light had been a small part of a much larger object.
