REPORT OF THE INVESTIGATING COMMITTEE OF THE C.I.0.V.I. (INVESTIGATING CENTER OF
UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS) ON THE CASE WHICH OCCURRED 13 OCTOBER 1958

The C.1I.0.V.I., in the present case, first gives a summing up of the data
available at the time because this is a new, exclusive and unprecedented phenomenon
and further information cannot be forwarded until the phenomenon is repeated.
Nevertheless, we think it necessary to point out that:

1) All the data in the observation files agree fundamentally.

2) That some of the observers agree that the trajectory of the object followed
a curving line along the firmament.

3) That there is a very curious thing about the reports on the object, they all
agree as to the size of the luminous headlight, that did not change all along the
trajectory that was almost from horizon to horizon. This would be a characteristic
of an object travelling in the outer space. But the curious part is that the ob-
servers in Montevideo say that "it passed over the zenith" while the ones in Pando
say "it passed over Montevideo." The observation of an object travelling in the
outer space would not justify such different reports on an object seen at such a
short distance (Montevideo - Pando).

4) As regards the intermission of the light, it could be a luminous headlight
on an object rotating on itself.
