SUMMARY (Enter concise summary of report. Give significance in final one-sentence paragraph. List inclosures at lower left. Begin text of report on AF Form 112a.)
An unidentified flying object was sighted at about 1800Z 29 October 1954
by members of the crew of C-47D aircraft 43-49219 flying an airway Amber 2
between Clark Field, Philippine Islands, and Kadena Air Base, Okinawa,
Ryukyus Islands. The objeet was long and narrow, bright blue (like the
exhaust flame of a reciprocating engine at cruising mixture), and curved with
the leading edge lower than the trailing edge. According to one observer,
this object had deep orange sparks emanating from both front and rear. The
sparks from the rear were in a definite pattern while those in front extended
out in a haphazard manner. (See Diagram I)
The object, sighted from an altitude of 9000 feet at 21050'N/123030'E,
was about 15 miles distant from the C-47 at about 1100 o'clock position (see
Diagram II) and about 2000 feet lower than flight altitude of the C-47.
