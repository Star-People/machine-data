Chester L. Brown Sightings:
Brown's sighting of the Bballoon" began at 0915-0920
PST and lasted until 0930-0935. They were made from a spot
on spokane's "South H111", 117 25W 47 39N. A telescope was
used. It was seen at about 30 degrees north of due east and
appeared to be 40-50 degrees from the horizontal. The object
did not drift. Brown claimed that he has seen weather balloons
before and that this object was not a weather balloon. It
changed shape periodically, elongating, and then returning to
its round shape. Brown saw no netting and nothing suspended
from the object. He described it as dlearly transparent and it
had a "tit" on the bottom. Brown estimated that the object was
over Felts Field (see chart) and that on the basis of such a
location would have been 8 to 10 ft in size. (Note: One "fix"
as shown on chart comes close to Felts Field).
