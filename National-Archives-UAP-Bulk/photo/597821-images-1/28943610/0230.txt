‘Fireball’ Geis a New Twist,
Nevada Has Blast and Blaze

Yesterday morning’s mysterious fireball, viewed over
a vast area in Utah, Idaho, Oregon, Washington and Nevada,
was still just as much a mystery today. ~

Reports of witnesses varied from a “green pea fireball
followed by a long orange streamer” to a “bright orange
fireball with a gaseous light blue smoke trail.”

People who know about meteors, including Charles
Osmond of Weber college and Dr. R. N. Thomas of the
University of Utah, agreed it had to be awfully big.

A new twist was injected this morning when'W. S.
Hobbs, assistant trainmaster at Montello, Nev., for the

Southern Pacifie railroad, reported many railroaders and ‘

others heard a heavy explosion about 10:30 a. m. yester-
day and saw a column of smoke and dust hanging in the sky.

Montello is about 112 miles almost due west of Ogden
as the crow flies. Hobbs said the gravish-blue cloud, outlined
with yellow dust remained for several minutes, then gradu-
ally drifted away. ‘

He said there was no known blasting in the area at the
time. . ,

tello.. They could find, however, no evidence of a meteor.
