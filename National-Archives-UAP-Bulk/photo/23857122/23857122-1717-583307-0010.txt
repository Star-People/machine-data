As well as I can recall, the hour was between 2000 and 2100 MST
on 5 August 1953.
The exact size of this craft would be a little hard to explain but
considering the altitude it seemed to be at, judging that from its!
apparent relations to the contours of the surrounding earth, I would
say it was between twenty and thirty feet in diameter, five to ten
thousand feet high.
It was very definitely shaped, perfectly round, looking very much
like a mommouth discus.
The crafts' color was a very light blue-green like that given off by
an intense flame, white heat.
I would say the speed of the eraft was approaching that of sound.
I say this because of the few seconds that I saw this craft. I saw it's
wake or path as well. It went out all at once, as if a light had been
clicked off. This craft was flying a true course from north east to south
west, almost parallel to the earths surface, dropping slightly, no more
than three degrees at the most. The nearest point to me was about four
and one half miles.
