ASTRONOMY
Amateur Discovers
Two New Comets
See Front Cover
A BRITISH SCHOOL teacher and amateur
astronomer has reported sighting two new
comets.
Comet Alcock 1959f, as one is called
(named after its discoverer, George Al-
cock), is the brightest comet seen in several
years. It was first seen on Aug. 30.
The photograph on the cover of this
Week'S SCIENCE
NEWS LETTER is a ten-
minute exposure of the comet, taken at the
U.S. Naval Observatory, Flagstaff, Ariz., on
Tuesday morning, Sept. 1. The short lines
going across the picture are stars. They are
trailed because the plate was moved during
exposure to follow the comet's motion.
Comet 1959e, the other comet discovered
by Mr. Alcock, is reported to be not as
bright as Comet 1959f.
It also lacks the tail
observed on Comet 1959f.
