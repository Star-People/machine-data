WASHINGTON, D. C.

November 1936

The enclosed shows the interest of the public in the future trend of
aircraft.
el Do you realize that it was the invention of the steam engine, making the
railroads possible a hundred years ago, that relieved a great world depression and
ushered in a new era of wealth and prosperity? Do you realize that the invention

of the automobile thirty-five years ago rendered a similar service to mankind? Do

you know that half of the cost of railroading is the road bed, and that we spend
in the United States alone one thousand million dollars annually for highways
travelled by the auto?

Do you realize that a very large portion of the world has neither rail-
roads nor highways as yet? Now the roads are all built for the Gray Goose. Nature
provides the highway. She furnishes gravity, which will be most of the power used
to travel the airways when man flies like a bird.

' The introduction of such a system of transportation will render all other
forms obsolete, because transportation is the life blood of commerce, and all com-
petitive industry and commerce are compelled to use the cheapest transportation in
order to survive.

Now the cheaper the transportation the greater dividends it pays. That
is why great fortunes were made by the early investors in rail and auto stocks.
Recently a famous writer made the statement that the first capable man acting on
a THOROUGH REALIZATION of the flying machines' future would build for his investors
such a fortune as would make the Hills, Harringtons and Vanderbilts look like small
financial crumbs.

We predict that our airplane will usher in a greater ERA of wealth and
industry than any previous form of transportation. One thing is certain -- that
fortunes must be built upon new ideas, new inventions and new conditions.

We maintain that our invention is already 99% proven. Each test has
disclosed some structural weakness that has been overcome with experiment. Since
there are no formulas for a radically new invention, experiment is our only guide.
We have stepped up our power as our weight increased. We now use a modern air-
cooled airplane engine of merit.

As soon as our structure proves strong and reliable enough, we shall
make news reels of a short flight. When that occurs, we believe that our stock
will be in demand at not less than fifty or a hundred dollars a share. Until that
time you may buy for yourself, your family or friends at ten cents a share.

Sincerely,

THE GRAY GOOSE AIRWAYS, Inc.

Jonathan E. Caldwell, Secy. & Gen. Mer.
