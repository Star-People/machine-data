After an hour of peeling our tires we

We twisted down out of a low range
of mountains and were going flat out

finely tuned yellow Lincoln sponsored by

The road was narrow and a hill rose up

I glanced down at the roller map

It was bouncing at the 125 mark. “This

“Start getting off of it.”

This was the turn I told you about in

come out. It was then the heavy car
twirled around in a dizzy arc and shot
off the cliff like a rocket.

My eyes were closed. I held my breath

It was up in the sky again. I was hang-

I thought my head would cave in with

Then there was a strange quiet. I
opened my eyes. We were in a cloud
of dust. My body felt OK. There was
a pin prick of blood on my thumb. I
looked at Billy. He was bent over the
wheel. He started moving. My door
had kicked off on the way down. A big
hole yawned up at me. “Don’t get out
Billy,’ I mumbled. “We've still got a
long ways to go.”

Vuky groaned. “Is the sonofabitch
going to start jumpin’ again?”
