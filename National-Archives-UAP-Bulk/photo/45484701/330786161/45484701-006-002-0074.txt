SYNOPSIS
Investigation requested by CO, Lowry A Base. Unidentified fast
moving flame or light was reported to have been observed by Lt. A. F. NABB,
1859 hours, 18 Jan 50 while flying in vicinity of Denver.
disclosed WEBB and six (6) other pilots flying in four aireraft observed
similar flame or light,at times varying slightly. All observers described
flame or light in similar terms and gave its course generally as southwesterly.
All four military aireraft were airborne in different locations and on
various headings when the observations were made by the pilots, Lowry Radar
inoperative, 1800-2080, 18 Jan 50. Lowry APB Training & Operations personnel
advised they had been notified of experimental airorart in area, 18 Jen 50.
Observations plotted and continuation of course suggest flame or light
heading towards Holloman AFB, New Mexico.
No ground observations of the
flame reported, feather conditions clear, visibility unlimited.
