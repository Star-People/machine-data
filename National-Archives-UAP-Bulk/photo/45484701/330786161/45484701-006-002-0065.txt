communication from Captain GEORGE W. GERMAN, AO 671740, Operations
Officer, Flight Service, Lowry Air Force Base, Colorado, advising that
between 1300 and 1310 hours he had observed 6 or 7 objects flying at a
very high altitude and rate of speed and that he did not believe the
objects were conventional type aircraft.
Special Agent EDWARD L. RUSH and M. L. McNABB conducted inter-
views with personnel of the 3933rd Radar Bomb Squadron (Detachment 3),
who advised that at about 1300 hours on 9 July a B-36 aircraft was in
the vicinity of Lowry Air Force Base flying at an altitude of approxi-
mately 30,000 feet and was working a radar and radio jamming problem.

jects had been sighted on the radar scopes at that time, However, the
jamming devises being used by the B-36 aircraft were very effective.
