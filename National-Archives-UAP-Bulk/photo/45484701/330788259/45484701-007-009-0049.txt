1. SYNOPSIS: Red and white light high in the sky observed
on the north side of Chicago, coming from the northwest about 21))5
hours, 12 July 1952. (OSI - A-2, Confidential)

2. DETAILS: This office was telephonically advised by Lt.
Colonel Pennepacker, Duty Officer, Fifth Army Headquarters, that
he had received information telephonically from Miss R. Lee, 5516
North Kenmore Avenue, Chicago, that she had sighted an unnatural
object resembling a red and white object in the sky over the 5500
block on North Kenmore Avenue (approximately six (6) miles north
of the downtown section of Chicago). Miss Lee related that between
2145 and 2150 hours, while walking south on Kenmore Avenue, she
sighted a white light in the sky which was coming from the north-
west. She advised that it resembled a star and that upon looking
directly at it the light gave a reddish appearance. She stated that
there was no flash or noise and that the object made a slow arc and
flew west very slowly in a straight line.

3. ACTION: Two (2) copies of instant report being forwarded
