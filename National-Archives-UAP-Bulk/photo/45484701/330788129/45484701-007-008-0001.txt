1. Reference is made to Air Force Regulation 200-2, dated
5 February 1958, concerning unidentified flying objects, and oral
conversation between Lt Colonel JOHN E. LITTLE, Executive
Officer, 2596th Air Base Squadron, and Special Agent SALVADOR
DEL VALLE, this District, on 4 February 1959.
On 4 February 1959, Mr. ORLAND W. COTTINGTON,
112th CIC Detachment, Dallas, Texas, telephonically advised that
Mr. and Mrs. FLOYD A. FERRIS, JR., residents of 325 East
Johnson Street, Wilmer, Texas, had reported the sighting of an
unidentified flying object approximately two (2) miles north of
Wilmer, Texas. FERRIS stated that while he and his wife were
driving in a northerly direction on U.S. Highway 75, at the outskirts
of Wilmer, Texas, they observed a bright fiery red round object,
which was traveling in a vertical direction, at an undetermined
speed, at an approximate angle of ten (10) degrees to the right of
the road. The time of this observation was between 0645 gh
00 50,
according to FERRIS, who added that this object had gone out
his view at an angle and elevation not specified.
