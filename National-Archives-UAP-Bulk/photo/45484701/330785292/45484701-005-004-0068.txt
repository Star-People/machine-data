Spot Intelligence Report, DO #9, 25 Nov 50, Subj: Report of Unconventional
Aircraft
In Meridian, Maj. B. D. Stephens of the Mississippi Air
National Guard said he chased an unidentified object for
65 miles at 300 miles an hour, but never got near it.
He said it did not have a vapor trail and that it re-
sembled the edge of a bright tilted plate, bar or
flattened out new moon.
Two mechanics at Key Field, in Meridian watched the
light with binoculars and said it could have been vapor
trails from a B-36.
Mrs. Fugenia Massey 24, who resides between Jackson and
Clinton, said she and other members of her family saw the
object about 4:45 p.m., They said it was flying at a very
high altitude and traveling very fast diredtly west.
She added that she could not see the front of the object
but the rear end looked like a cylinder of flame about
five or six feet long.
Mrs. Massey said she called the observation tower at the
Jackson Air Base who confirmed her report of seeing the
strange object, but officials there would not attempt to
identify it."
