2. Major EDWARD A. DOTY, 12523A, Assistant Chief, Electronics

released near White Sands Proving Ground on 22 January 1951. The balloon

for a number of hours, and the C-45 flown by Captains SPRADLEY and COCKER

were quite new in the balloon field and he believed that it was their
first tracking mission. DOTY further advised that these balloons usually
have several small metal instrument containers hanging from them and often,
at very high altitudes, an inexperienced observerwill think he sees some-

balloon, since MC ALEESE is considered well qualified in balloon work.
