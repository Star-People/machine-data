At 2330 hours, 18 May 1959, he turned off his television set and went

"one-half the size of a house" in circumference, and had four golden points
protruding evenly from it, with each point being approximately the "size of
a pine tree." He observed this object for approximately ten seconds but
did not see any motion or hear any noise coming from it. The object gradu-
ally faded out and while doing so changed many colors.
