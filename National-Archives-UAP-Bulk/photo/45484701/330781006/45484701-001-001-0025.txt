11. SUMMARY OF IMFORMATION

Mr. Quinn was contacted by Mr. Ed W. Gurband, 2518 Sacramento
Street, San Francisco, California, on 29 August 1949, with the infor-
mation that he (Gurband) had sighted a total of nine (9) flying
saucers on 1 August 1949, while fishing in the main shipping channel
of San Francisco Bay, San Francisco, California. Mr. Gurband stated
that there were several witnesses besides himself. Mr. Gurband
further reported that on 29 August 1949, he and other persons (number
unknown) sighted a falling parachute which was shredded and raveled.
