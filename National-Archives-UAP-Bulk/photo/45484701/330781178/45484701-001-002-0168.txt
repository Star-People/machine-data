on Sunday night, Apr11 3rd. 1949, Mr. M.C. Janison, patrolmen
of the Border Patrol,
U.S. Immigration Service,
came to my hone to
report the following:
A Mr./Slover and a Mr. Curley Talters reported to Mr. Jemison
that on Thursday, 31 March 1949, Mr. Slover heard a plane overhead
and missing. He next heard a very heavy explosion and thought that
a plane had crashed. The time of day or night is not fixed in this
second hand report.
The following day, Mr, Slover and Mr. Walters rode on horse-
back to the site of the explosion and described a swath in the brush
and timber culminating in a huge crater. Mr. Slover described a piece
of metal about 12 inches in diameter and pointed. He felt it was sone
kind of a "mar head" for a guided missile. Nothing resembling a plane
was found.
The site of the explosion is described as wild mountainous country,
about ten miles east of Rodeo, New Mexico and about five miles fron the
ranches of We Slover and Mr, Walters.
The nearest emergency landing
field by plane is said to be at Rodeo. The site is about seventy-five
miles by automobile from Douglas,
