TWX from DO #10

DO 10 received information from Houston Division, FBI, that three or four
weeks ago, while ship SST KEY TRADER was at sea, the first mate and an
able-bodied seaman, not further identified, observed a rocket land in the
water next to the ship. The incident and its location are recorded in the
ship's log. Incident reportedly occurred same evening that a rocket
supposedly carrying a mouse was fired from Cape Canaveral. This ship
departed Houston, 21 May 1958. It is scheduled to arrive Seawaren, New
Jersey in 5 days (which would be 26 May 1958).

Col Moore
