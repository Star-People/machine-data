DETATIS :
The Vallejo News Chronicle, Vallejo, California
on Friday 1 September 1950 published photographs and an article regarding
an object described as a "large, wind current, observation kite" which
was found in the Jard of Mrs. ILLENE BRINK, 907 Amador Street, Vallejo,
California. The article stated that the "Kite" had a message attached
which requested the finder to notify an airman in a US AF. Squadron
at Treasure Island. Sgt. H. C. ADORNIK, AF-17249660, Detachment "E",
3903rd Radar Bomb Strike Station, USAF, Treasure Island Naval Statior
was contacted on 12 September 1950 and stated the device found in Vallejo
was an instrument for determining the winds aloft in conjunction with
Radar (RAWIN) and that his organization usually sent them aloft twice a
day at 0800 and 1500 hours.
ADORNIK further stated that it was the
practice of the airman of the detachment to affix their name and the
address of the unit to the Device requesting the finder to notify same
as to the location at which the Device was found. ADORNICK advised
that prior to approximately 1 September 1950 that there was nothing on
the Device which identified the agency which released them, nowever,
that he had since rectified this and that a card with the following
statement is now affixed:
