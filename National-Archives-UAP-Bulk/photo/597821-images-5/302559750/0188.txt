Memo for the Record:
10 Jan 67
Call was received from Major Nyls, Selfridge APB, Michågan (ex-3115)
in regards to a UrO sighting over Lake St. Clair. He said that
photos were taken of the object by two men as they were out in their
back yard. The object was about a quarter of a mile away and
hovered before speeding away. He said that he would sent copies
of the pictures as soon as he could with camera data. Major Nyls
said that he had contacted SAFOI and told them of the sighting.
The sighting should have a lot of public interest.
