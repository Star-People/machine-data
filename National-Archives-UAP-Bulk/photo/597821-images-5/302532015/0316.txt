Received from Colonel Welms, Command Post, 10 April 64, 114Q
SUBJECT: Unidentified Flying Objects
Original Message
MCC, Norad advised that there were 6 to 12 unidentified flying
objects at 30 miles East of Merced, California. Radar picked up 12
objects at altitudes 60,000 ft., 90,000 ft., and higher elevations.
F-106'a were scrambled at Castle Air Force Base. There were no
results because of high altitudes. They are checking the possibility
of sending U-2'3.
Later Sent to Air Staff
Objects were following a 50 mile race-track pattern. F-106's vere
flying a 99,099 ft. altitude. Pilots locked on to some of the objects
but could not keep the lock. Norad said they were sending 2 more LINK
gircraft with pilots in pressure suita.
