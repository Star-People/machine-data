1. PURPOSE: This report is in response to Work Order Number 68-110 submitted by Lieutenant
Colonel Quintanilla (TDPT) requesting analysis of a photograph taken near Acton, Texas,
19 November 1968, to determine the identity of a purported UFO.

2. ANALYSIS:

a, Examination and technical analysis of the print and corresponding negative furnished
by Lieutenant Colonel Carter indicate that the most probable explanation of the image in
question is that it is a film processing or emulsion defect. One evident possible cause is
the abnormal appearance of the emulsion side of the negative at the location of the image
in question. There appears to be some form of foreign substance of high reflectivity covering
the area around the image. The "spot" would be impossible to analyze without destroying the
image in question. It appears as though there may have been a chemical deposit on or in the
emulsion which prevented exposure in that area and then retarded development when it was
dissolved by the processing liquids. Corroboration of this explanation might have been accom-
plished if the film wrapper had been available for analysis.

_within the camera. The facts are as follows:

(1) The photographer did not actually see the "object".
