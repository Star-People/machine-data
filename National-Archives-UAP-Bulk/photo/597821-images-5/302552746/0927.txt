DAYTON, OHIO
6 Jul 66
7/0155Z
Officer Baldridge of The Dayton Police Department called on 7 July

1055 pm.

mentioned in the newspaper. The newsclipping stated that the Air
Force at Wright-Patterson said that the object a lady had observed
was a meteor streaking across the sky. The officer observed the

second observance. Arc headed toward the ground, it tailed out

a rather bright meteor.

of a meteor.
