Patuxent NAS, Maryland
29 December 1964
Based on results of an investigation by the Patuxent NAS and analysis
of information supplied to the Air Force by the Navy, it has been
determined that false radar targets were mistakenly reported as uniden-
tified flying objects on 29 Dec 64. Study of the shape and illumination
of the return indicates that they were created by some other electronic
device within the station or were caused by an intermittent abnormality
within the circuitry of the radar set itself. This conclusion has been
further verified by a check with an independent radar complex at Patuxent
NAS and with adjacent radar sector centers on the East coast which revealed
no corresponding radar return on that date.
