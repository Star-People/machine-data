NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

WALLOPS STATION, WALLOPS ISLAND, VIRGINIA 23337
TELEPHONE: VALLEY 4-3411 - EXTS. 584 and 579

FOR RELEASE: May 2, 1969

Release No. 69-9

BLACK BRANT IIIB ROCKET LAUNCHINGS

A more powerful Black Brant IIIB rocket, built by Bristol
Aerospace of Winnipeg, was launched yesterday at 2:26 p.m. EDT
by NASA at the Wallops Island, Virginia launch site.

‘he Canadian rocket, using a higher impulse solid propellant,
tarried a 112-pound payload to an altitude of about 135 miles. The
present Black Brant ILI is capable of carrying an 88-pound payload
to 114 miles.

Two of these uprated rockets were scheduled to be fired from
Wallops Island fer vehicle testing. The first was successfully
launched at 1:57 p.m. on February 28.

NASA contracted with the company to modify the existing Black
Brant IfLL for a more powerful propellant to enable the rocket to
reach altitudes in excess of 200 kilometers with nominal scientific
payload experiment weights.

Ralph Bullock was the Bristol Aerospace Project Manager, Arthur

A. Rudmann was the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center's Vehicle Man-

Island Project Engineers.
