SUBJ: Investigation of Reported UFO Landing Site
TO: ETW (Lt J.S. Alexander)

1. Per your arrangement, I met Lt Goettle, MacDill AFB,
at the WWJB Radio Station in Brooksville at approximately
1130, Saturday, 6 March 1965.

2. Lt Goettle was accompanied by another individual, an
Air Force Sergeant. We drove as near to the reported
site as possible and continued on foot. After a period
of about an hour, and with the assistance of a local
resident (in his Model A Ford "woods-buggy") we arrived
at the site.

3. It was evident that the area had received rain and an
indeterminable number of visitors since the original report
was made. Therefore, the indentations or holes which
supposedly represented the imprint of the landing legs
were partially "filled-in", yet their location was very
evident. The previously photographed footprints has been
Obliterated, thus, no observable evidence of their exis-
tence remained.

4. The area was examined closely, the spacing and compass
Orientation of the holes were measured and a scintillation
counter survey made of the ground within the four-hole
array and surrounding area.

5. No opinion was solicited or will be offered as to the
authenticity of the sighting or the reliability of the
observer, for I did not meet or question Mr. Symp. iI
would prefer to limit my discussion to factual evidence
and not delve into conjecture.
