Enclosed are UFO Witness Profile Reports for the months September
1968 through December 1968 inclusive. An Annual Witness Profile
report comprising of an overview of the 12 monthly reports has
been included in your copy of the NICAP MASS SUBCOM ANNUAL REPORT
for 1968.

The UFO Witness Profile Reports are not intended to be exhaustive
surveys but rather are a means by which one can quickly obtain a
summary of the type of witnesses who are submitting UFO Reports
to the NICAP MASS SUBCOM. The witnesses are referenced to their
respective UFO Reports by a "filing number" in order that further
information might be obtained as required.

Present Education means a distinct educational unit presently held
by the witness. A unit must be fully completed before one is given
credit. For example, one might be a Senior in College but would
still be listed as a High School Graduate until a degree had been
awarded. Due to space limitations there is no distinction between
the sexes. The Occupation Other covers a variety of "other-than-
listed" occupations such as None, Housewife, Minister, Artist, etc.
Encircled items connected by lines indicate occupation/experience
plurality, i.e., one might be an Engineer but hold a private pilots

license.
