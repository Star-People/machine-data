BACKGROUND:
Several days after the sighting, I received a letter informing me
about it from a friend of mine who is acquainted with the witness.
I assigned Investigator Eric Thorsos to conduct an interview with
the witness.
The attached report represents the results of his
investigation.
CHAIRMAN'S COMMENTS:
Although the sighted phenomena has some of the characteristics of
a searchlight, the factors of weather condition, sighting time and
duration, maneuvers and the associated blue and amber lights seem
to rule this explanation out.
The only bright astronomical object
in that sector of the sky was Sirius but it was only a few degrees
above the horizon and would not appear almost the angular size of
a full moon.
It would not appear to alternately change shape nor
would it blink out upon the approach of a conventional aircraft.
The sighting does resemble those of glowing "cloud cigar" type
reports. However, no egress or ingress of other objects was noted
when the cigar would have been in a vertical mode unless the blue
and amber lights were other objects and not connected to the larger
object. I phoned the witness to re-question her
on this point but
her impression was that the object was round - "like a big button" -
and that it was revolving so that its face and edge were alternately
seen. She felt that the lights were "hanging from the object" or
that they were connected to it rather than being seperate objects.
She described the object as glowing the brightest and whitest white
she had ever seen - much brighter than a full moon.
