HEADQUARTERS EIGHTH AIR FORCE
OFFICE OF THE COMMANDING GENERAL
FORT WORTH, TEXAS

9 December 1947

Major General Clements McMullen
Strategic Air Command

Andrews Field

Washington 20, D. CS.

Dear General McMullen:

I have intended to mention to you several times since General
Rawling's visit that we considered his visit most instructive and
although we thought we were doing fairly well in a comparative cost
analysis we found (at least I did in particular) that we didn't
know too much about the subject... I was unable to explain without
considerable after-study several things that I knew at the time
were wrong as you will note from the inclosed letter to General
Rawlings. He asked me to send him what the actual story was when
I found it out.

As I told you in our telephone conversation yesterday, the
B-29 airplane situation is apparently very bad since they are now
a month behind in their supply and no immediate solution seems
forthcoming.

Hutchinson called after you called yesterday and stated that
in the telephone conversation with Roswell they found out a great
many things other than morning reports that would have to be
changed and that USAF had agreed to our solution. Certainly glad
that they decided to do so because the change would have been
almost. disastrous and physical compliance with their original
order would have been extremely difficult. lihat, of course, we
should have done was to have Air Forces delay their original order
for uso

General, I would sure be interested in knowing what General
Kenney worked out with General McNerney on the gasoline situation.
I certainly am worried about that because I am very mucn afraid
that if the situation continues that we are leading into an epi-
sode of either grounding planes or fires in the air, or both.
Incidentally, we are submitting some projects now toward revision
of aqua systems in order to meet the possibility of being supplied

with various octane rating gasolines.
