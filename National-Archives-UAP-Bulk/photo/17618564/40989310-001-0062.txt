Jud Roberts reports another call from Washington, which he says he

It is possible Whitmore and Roberts are remembering the same call, the
differences in their recollections a consequence of time and the vagaries of
human memory and perception. It is also possible there was more than one
call In any event, the interview was not aired, and according to Whitmore,
the wire recording and the recorder on which it was made have long since
been discarded[52]
