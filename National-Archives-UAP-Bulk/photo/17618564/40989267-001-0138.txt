A mirror was mounted under the main telescope. Its rotation in elevation

1.2 Design of Experiments

Scintillation, seeing, and interference by absorption of light by water vapor
were to be studied by a photoelectric photometer, motion picture camera and
infrared photometer, respectively. The above instruments were housed ina
compartment mounted on the back of the telescope (see Fig. 1). They were
mounted on a plate which was rotated to position the particular instrument in the
focal plane of the telescope. Figure 2 shows the configuration of the experiments.
