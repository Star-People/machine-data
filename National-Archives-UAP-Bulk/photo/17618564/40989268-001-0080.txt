With that background we should now be ready to design a crash pad. We start
with the parameters: the cushioning material, the payload weight, the peak
allowable acceleration, and the expected impact velocity; and first determine the

"Ideal'' pad is usable if your stress-strain curve is essentially rectangular, as
most curves for paper honeycomb are.
