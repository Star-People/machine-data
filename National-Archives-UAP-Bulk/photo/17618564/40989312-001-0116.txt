A Look Into the Future

Athel has brought together two scientific disciplines—oceanography and meteorology.
Often during his career, he has made predictions which frequently come true. Athel, it
has been said, “has an exceptional gift for pulling into his mind an encyclopedia of
assorted facts; and, synthesizing from them, better approaches by which science can serve
humanity.” In this chapter, rather than taking a look at the future as a soothsayer would,
Athel has chosen to tell us about a dream of his.

“Dreams come quickly to reality if we work at making them come true. If we don’t
design and work, nightmares become reality. I want you to come with me on a little
exploratory trip, and not wait for the future. We can enjoy it now by planning it and
doing things toward that end. I would like to confine myself to “the dilemmas of
mankind.’

“The two greatest dilemmas of mankind, to my way of thinking, are overpopulation—
overgrazing the earth on one hand, and energy—because energy is the fundamental
currency of civilization.

“If we work towards stabilization of population and the increase of per capita energy, all
the stuff in between can be solved. However, if we cannot control our numbers, and I
mean self control in whatever way suits each person according to their religious and
moral tenets; then none of the solutions that I propose can really be effective in the long
run.

“If we cannot increase our energy production in new, clean and economical ways, then
even stabilizing population will be of no benefit.

and asked him what he thought about birth control. He looked up and said to the speaker
