senior officer quickly asked him to remove it “as was the American custom.” Lao Keh
politely removed his hat, but after the senior officer had left the mess, he stated that it
was we who were the guests there and wearing a hat to meals was the Chinese custom.

“If one could describe Mao’s success in one word, it would be “discipline.” I expect
