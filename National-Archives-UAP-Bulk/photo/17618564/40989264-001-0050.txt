(by Carl Sagan)
Unidentified fiving objects (UFO's) is the generic term for moving aerial or
celestial phenomena, detected visually or by radar, whose nature is not immedi
ately understood. Interest in these objects stems from speculation that some of
them are the products of civilizations beyond the earth, and from the psychologi-
cal insights into contemporary human problems that this interpretation provides.
Observations. Unidentified fving obiects have been described variously as
rapidly moving or hovering; disc-shaped, cigar-shaped, or ball-shaped; moving
silently or noisily; with a fiery exhaust, or with no exhaust whatever; accom-
pained by flashing lights, or uniformly glowing with a silvery cast. The diversity
of the observations suggests that UFO's have no common origin and that the use
of such terms as UFO's or "flying saucers" serves only to confuse the issue by
grouping generically a variety of unrelated phenomena.
