3.2 Resolver
The function of the resolver in the -p device is to derive azimuth and eleva-
tion servo-signals from the sun-sensor output. If the sun is off-center, a 50 cycle
A.C. signal is generated, which has its maximum value in the direction of the sun
image. After amplification, this signal is fed through magnetic reed switches into
the azimuth and elevation servo circuits (Figure 3). The switches have to be placed
in proper position in order to minimize cross-talk, which necessitates programming
of the resolver. The image of the sun on the sun-sensor is formed through reflec-
tion by the Dove-prism, the reflecting plane being the bisecting plane of the off-set
angle. (Figure 1) Upon displacement of the gondola in elevation, the sun moves in
the direction EL1, in a plane parallel to the vertical plane through Venus. The angle
