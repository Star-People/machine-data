The Beedy Ghject and Gther Reperts

Given the meteor-like description of the Woodys’ object, it is virtually certain
not to have been the same phenomenon seen by the Wilmots. It is possible
the Woodys saw the source of the debris and, perhaps, what was the disabled
craft reportedly found nearby. However, we have the same trajectory/debris-
field orientation problems here as with the Wilmot UFO. Moreover, the date
of the Woodys’ sighting is uncertain, although their subsequent experience
“two or three days later (definitely not the next day)" with soldiers stationed
along U.S. 285 north of Roswell suggests it was before Brazel's discovery[3]

The other reports of aerial object sightings theoretically linked with the
debris and crashed vehicle suffer essentially the same difficulties discussed
above. These have already been addressed in chapter 2.

The Sky Shee, the Earth Below

Unfortunately, we have only a highly tenuous, highly speculative link between
anything seen in the sky and what was found by Mac Brazel and, possibly,
elsewhere by the military. Only the Wilmot and Woody sightings offer any
reasonable basis for considering such a connection, and the facts so far
available permit only speculation. Once again, this time with respect to a
connection between things seen in the sky and things found on the earth, we
are left with no responsible choice but to suspend judgment.
