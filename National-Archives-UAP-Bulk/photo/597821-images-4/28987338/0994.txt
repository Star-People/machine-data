off the starboard bow as we approached. The cassette at this time
was clearly visible about 14 degrees off the Starboard Beam but seemed
in the same line or distance from the shop.
I was with the first Mate of the Ship at the time and I mentioned it to
him and said that the A/C flare was dying out, that the cassette light
was still visible for the recovery.
I thought nothing more of this, as I expectad this was routine for re-
